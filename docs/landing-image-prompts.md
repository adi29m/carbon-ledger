# Landing imagery

The three editorial photos in `public/images` were created with the built-in imagegen tool for this prototype. They are illustrative scenes, not photos of a real CarbonLedger customer or facility.

## Aluminium plant (`aluminium-plant.webp`)

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: premium B2B SaaS landing-page hero photograph, wide landscape. A real modern aluminium extrusion plant at dusk: neatly stacked silver aluminium profiles in the foreground, production hall lights glowing beyond, precise industrial architecture, subtle haze, no identifiable workers. Sophisticated editorial photography, architectural-magazine quality, believable materials and machinery, cinematic but realistic, deep forest green shadows and warm brass highlights. Composition leaves generous dark negative space on the left for website headline; the illuminated aluminium and plant activity sit mostly on the right. No text, no logo, no watermark, no data graphics, no futuristic fantasy objects.

## Aluminium production (`aluminium-production.webp`)

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: premium B2B SaaS landing-page editorial photo, landscape. A clean close view of silvery aluminium extrusions and precision manufacturing equipment in an Indian export-oriented factory, crisp geometric repeating lines, a worker's gloved hands only as scale (no faces), realistic polished metal texture, soft morning light entering industrial windows. High-end documentary photography, natural rather than staged, warm neutral and muted forest green palette. Compose with central subject and enough room for a crop in a 4:3 feature card. No text, no logo, no watermark, no data graphics.

## Export port (`export-port.webp`)

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: premium B2B SaaS landing-page editorial photo, landscape. International trade logistics at a port in India: orderly shipping containers and a cargo crane, late-afternoon golden light, sea in background, subtle sense of movement and scale, no readable container logos or text. High-end architectural documentary photography for a climate-compliance software brand, natural realistic detail, elegant warm neutral tones with deep evergreen shadows. Compose to work as a wide 16:9 website card. No text, no logo, no watermark, no people in focus, no futuristic effects.
